Preparing images

    A better image, a better inspection

    1. Start with the front and the back. Add all printed side panels.
    2. Fill the frame with the label. Keep the camera parallel to the surface.
    3. Move the light or package to remove glare. Avoid flash on plastic.
    4. Add a close-up of dotted MRP, manufacturing, expiry and batch markings.
    5. Include a verified scale card when checking physical text height.

    Image quality checks offer practical guidance. They cannot prove that hidden or cropped information is absent.

    How a finding becomes a decision

    OCR reads the image. Versioned rules test the declaration. The inspector reviews the evidence and a separate supervisor approves the record.
