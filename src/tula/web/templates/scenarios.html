Every row synthesises a label, runs the full pipeline, and asserts that named clauses reach named verdicts. A row passes only if every expectation matches and the measurement contains the geometry that was actually drawn.

The last six rows are the important ones. Everything above them hands the system a complete, legible label — which is exactly the blind spot that let real photographs produce fifty non-conformities against compliant national brands. Those six hand it what a real inspection produces instead: one face of a package, a frame that failed, a citizen's phone. The property under test there is the absence of an accusation.
